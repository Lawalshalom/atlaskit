# Code of Conduct

AtlasKit is built for developers across Nigeria and beyond. We want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions and events) to feel welcome, whatever their language, background, experience or identity.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being kind, patient and respectful, especially to newcomers and to people writing in a second language;
- welcoming questions and contributions in English, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo or Pidgin;
- giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully;
- crediting other people's work, data and ideas.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults or personal attacks;
- discrimination based on ethnicity, tribe, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or any other personal characteristic;
- mocking anyone's language, dialect or accent;
- publishing someone's private information without their permission;
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**), noting that it is a conduct report. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who does so.
